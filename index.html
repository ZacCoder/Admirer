CHAPTER 01 · THE INTRODUCTION 01
📝

Well... where do I begin?

Dear Miss,

First of all, congratulations! You have officially been selected as the reader of this very unofficial, highly unnecessary, and somewhat questionable publication.

I would like to inform you that this notebook was created by an admirer who apparently has too many things running through his head and decided that writing them down was a better idea than letting them remain there forever.

I could have simply sent a message. That would have been easier, shorter, and probably more convenient. But knowing myself, I would probably end up writing a very long paragraph anyway.

So here we are. A whole notebook. With chapters. Pages. Decorations. And probably more effort than a normal person would put into an apology.

Please don't judge the author too harshly. He's trying. HAHAHA.

— Your humble admirer ✎

CHAPTER 02 · A SMALL EXPLANATION 02
🖊️

Why did I make this?

Honestly, because there are things I should have said properly a long time ago.

Sometimes, I think I know what I want to say, but when the moment comes, I don't express it the way I intended. I end up being quiet, choosing the wrong words, or thinking that everything will somehow become okay on its own.

And that's not always fair to the person on the other side of the conversation.

I don't want this notebook to be another message that simply says sorry and expects everything to go back to normal.

I want to acknowledge the things I did wrong, the things I could have handled better, and the moments when my actions didn't match what I actually wanted to express.

Also, I wanted to do something a little different. Something that would make you smile, maybe laugh a little, and hopefully remind you that someone took the time to put these thoughts together.

Not the most ordinary notebook, I suppose.

CHAPTER 03 · THE FIRST APOLOGY 03
📓

About the times I went quiet.

I'm sorry for the times I didn't communicate properly.

There were moments when I became quiet because I didn't know what to say, because I thought I was giving things time, or because I was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the situation worse.

But I realize that silence can be confusing too. It can leave someone wondering what happened, what the other person is thinking, or whether they still matter enough to receive an explanation.

I shouldn't have expected you to understand everything I never actually explained. You cannot read my mind, unfortunately. Although that would make communication considerably easier. HAHAHA.

I should have communicated more clearly, even when things felt uncomfortable. I should have been honest about what was happening instead of leaving you to guess.

I'm sorry if my silence made you feel ignored, unimportant, or uncertain. That wasn't something you deserved.

I could have said something. I should have.

COMMUNICATION MATTERS 03 / 10
CHAPTER 04 · THINGS I SHOULD HAVE DONE 04
📌

I should have taken more initiative.

This is something I've been thinking about.

Sometimes, I ask what you want, what you prefer, or what you would like to do because I genuinely want to know. But I also understand that asking all the time isn't the same as making an effort to think ahead.

There are times when I should have made plans, suggested something, or simply taken the first step instead of waiting for everything to be figured out through another conversation.

I don't want you to feel like you always have to be the one thinking about what happens next. I should be capable of making thoughtful decisions too.

And no, I don't mean that I'll suddenly become a professional event planner overnight. Please lower your expectations just a little. I am still learning. HAHAHA.

But I can be more intentional. I can pay attention, remember the little things, and make an effort without always waiting to be told what to do.

Less waiting. More doing.

EFFORT SHOULD BE VISIBLE 04 / 10
CHAPTER 05 · THE MISUNDERSTANDINGS 05
🗒️

For the times I misunderstood.

I'm sorry for the times I misunderstood your words, your feelings, or the things you were trying to communicate.

Sometimes, I get caught up in my own thoughts. I overthink what something means, make assumptions, or react before I fully understand the situation.

I know that listening is more than just waiting for my turn to respond. It means actually trying to understand what someone is saying, even when it isn't easy to hear.

I also know that good intentions don't automatically make every action right. I might mean well, but that doesn't erase how something could have made you feel.

So I'm sorry for the moments when I didn't listen carefully enough, when I made things more complicated, or when I failed to understand your side.

I don't want every disagreement to become a competition about who is right. Sometimes, understanding matters more than winning an argument.

A little more listening. A little less assuming.

UNDERSTANDING TAKES EFFORT 05 / 10
CHAPTER 06 · THE LITTLE DETAILS 06
✏️

I should pay attention.

I know that thoughtful gestures aren't always about spending money or giving something expensive.

Sometimes, it's about remembering a detail, noticing when something matters to someone, or doing something small because you genuinely thought of them.

I don't want to be someone who only asks what you like but never remembers the answer.

I want to become better at noticing the little things, remembering conversations, and paying attention to what makes your day a little better.

I may not remember every single detail perfectly. My memory occasionally behaves like an old computer with too many tabs open. Please forgive the hardware. HAHAHA.

But I shouldn't use that as an excuse. I can make a conscious effort to remember what matters.

The little things aren't little when they matter to someone.

PAY ATTENTION TO WHAT MATTERS 06 / 10
CHAPTER 07 · AN HONEST REFLECTION 07
📖

I'm not going to make excuses.

I know that writing an apology is easy. Making meaningful changes is the part that actually matters.

I could fill this entire notebook with explanations, reasons, and promises. I could make every mistake sound like an unfortunate accident.

But I don't want to do that.

I have things to improve. I have habits to work on. I have moments I wish I had handled differently.

I am still learning how to communicate better, how to take initiative, how to be more thoughtful, and how to handle difficult conversations without making them harder.

I won't pretend that one notebook magically fixes everything. It doesn't. Unfortunately, there is no save button for real life. I checked. HAHAHA.

But I can start by acknowledging what needs to change and actually putting effort into it.

No perfect promises. Just genuine effort.

ACCOUNTABILITY BEFORE EXCUSES 07 / 10
CHAPTER 08 · SOMETHING I APPRECIATE 08
🌷

A few words of appreciation.

Beyond the apologies, I also want to acknowledge the person reading this.

I appreciate the conversations, the laughter, the little moments, and even the ordinary days that somehow became memorable.

I admire your determination, the things you work hard for, and the way you continue moving forward even when things get tiring.

I hope you never forget that your achievements deserve recognition. You don't have to minimize your accomplishments or act like everything you do is ordinary.

And yes, this is the part where the admirer becomes slightly embarrassing and starts giving compliments.

Please remain calm. This is a documented event. There is no need to call the authorities. HAHAHA.

Seriously, though, I hope you continue to recognize your own worth and give yourself credit for everything you've worked for.

Keep doing your best, Miss.

A NOTE OF APPRECIATION 08 / 10
CHAPTER 09 · MOVING FORWARD 09
📎

What I hope to do better.

I don't want to end this notebook by simply listing everything I regret. I also want to think about what I can do differently from here.

I want to communicate more openly, even when I don't have the perfect words prepared.

I want to be more thoughtful instead of assuming that good intentions are enough.

I want to take initiative, make plans, and show effort through actions instead of relying only on explanations.

I want to listen without immediately becoming defensive, and understand before reacting.

Most importantly, I want to respect your feelings and your decisions. You deserve honesty, consideration, and communication.

I know improvement takes time, and I won't ask you to believe every word just because I wrote it. Actions will always speak more clearly.

I'll let my actions do the talking.

A PROMISE TO DO BETTER 09 / 10
CHAPTER 10 · THE LAST PAGE 10
✉️

And that's everything... for now.

Well, Miss, you've officially reached the last page.

Thank you for spending a little of your time reading this very unnecessarily long notebook.

I know I could have made a shorter message. I know I could have simply said sorry. But I wanted to give these thoughts a little more attention than that.

I'm genuinely sorry for the moments I could have handled better, for the things I failed to communicate, and for the times my actions didn't reflect the consideration I should have shown.

I hope you know that this apology isn't meant to pressure you into responding a certain way. You don't owe me an immediate reply or a particular reaction.

I simply wanted to say these things properly, with honesty and a little creativity along the way.

Take care of yourself, keep smiling, keep doing the things you love, and don't forget to give yourself a little rest between all those responsibilities.

Sincerely,
Your admirer.
